# When to sell — simple rules

There is no exact time to sell. Anyone who tells you "this stock will hit X on date Y" is guessing. What you **can** do is set rules for yourself **in advance** and follow them.

## Rule 1: Set a profit target before you buy

Before buying, decide: "I'm happy if this returns 20%, and I'll sell some at that point."

When you add a holding in this app, you can set a **Target %**. The signal **Profit target reached** fires when the position hits that return. It doesn't auto-sell — that's still your decision — but the reminder is the point.

## Rule 2: Set a stop loss

Decide the maximum loss you'll tolerate before you buy. Common rules: 15% or 20%. If price drops below that, sell without arguing with yourself.

This protects you from the most common mistake: holding a losing stock hoping it "comes back" until it loses 50% or 80%.

## Rule 3: Trim near 52-week highs

When a stock has run a long way and is within 3% of its 52-week high, it often consolidates or pulls back. **Selling part of your position** (e.g. 25%) locks in profit while letting the rest run.

The **Near 52-week high** signal flags this.

## Rule 4: Watch the moving averages

When the price falls **below both the 120-day and 180-day average**, it's a downtrend signal. This doesn't mean "sell everything immediately" — but it's a moment to:
- Re-examine the fundamentals.
- Decide if you want to hold through the downtrend or step out and re-enter later.

The **Below moving averages** signal flags this.

## Rule 5: Strong dividend = usually a hold

If a stock pays a strong dividend (yield above 8%) consistently, the income alone is reason to hold even through some price volatility. Don't sell just because the price wobbles.

The **Strong dividend** signal flags this.

## Rule 6: Fundamentals stretched vs sector

If a stock's P/E is much higher than its sector's average, the premium needs justification — usually unusually fast growth or a special advantage. When the premium is large and the growth story weakens, the stock falls back to fair value.

The **Valuation stretched vs sector** signal flags this.

## What NOT to do

- **Don't sell on a single bad day.** NEPSE moves a lot day to day. Look at trends, not single sessions.
- **Don't chase tips.** "X will hit Rs Y by next month" — almost always wrong.
- **Don't average down a falling stock without rechecking fundamentals.** A bad business gets worse, not cheaper.
- **Don't sell purely because the price went up.** If fundamentals still support holding, riding a winner is one of the best things you can do.

## The honest truth

Nobody predicts exact tops and bottoms. The goal is not perfect timing — it's making decisions you can defend, before emotions get involved. The signals in this app exist to help with that.
